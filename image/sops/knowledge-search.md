# Knowledge search

Use [Search knowledge](/actions/search_knowledge.py) after the issue has a clear summary. Prefer results matching the account area, product, severity, and symptoms.

Present the best matches with their title, source, and relevance. Separate historical evidence from a recommendation. If no useful result exists, say so and do not invent a root cause or resolution.
