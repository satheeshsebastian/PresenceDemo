# Human approval

Before creating or updating a CRM case, creating tasks, escalating, or producing a send-ready communication, show the proposed change and ask for explicit confirmation.

For high-severity, customer-wide, regulatory, legal, financial, or ambiguous issues, require human review and offer handoff. If an action fails, report the failure and do not pretend it completed.
