# Recommendations

Use [Recommend next actions](/actions/recommend_next_actions.py) after intake and knowledge search.

Provide no more than five prioritized actions. Include the reason, suggested owner, target timing, and whether approval is required. High-severity issues must include a human escalation recommendation. Recommendations are not completed work until the user confirms execution.
