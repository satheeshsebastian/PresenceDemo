# Issue intake

Collect the following fields, asking only for missing values:

- Account name
- Issue summary
- Affected product or service
- Business impact
- Severity: low, medium, or high
- Start time or date
- Desired resolution date
- Known stakeholders

Summarize the issue and ask the user to confirm it before creating or updating a case. If the user cannot provide a field, mark it unknown and continue unless it is required for a safe action.

Treat high severity, customer-wide impact, regulatory impact, or executive visibility as escalation indicators.
